1. Information Stored in the App · 应用内保存的信息

The app stores the following on your device only: play progress and rewards, and optional records that a parent or caregiver chooses to enter (mood before and after a session, a focus rating, and session duration). We do NOT collect names, photos, contacts, or location data, and the app does not require an account.

应用只在您的设备上保存以下内容：游玩进度与奖励，以及家长或照护者自愿输入的记录（练习前后的心情、专注程度和练习时长）。我们不收集姓名、照片、通讯录或位置信息，使用应用也无需注册账号。

2. Children's Privacy · 儿童隐私保护

Star Park is designed to be used by children together with a parent or caregiver. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools. The only third-party service the app uses is the subscription service described in Section 5, and purchases are made through a parent verification step.

Star Park 为儿童在家长或照护者陪伴下使用而设计。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。应用不含广告，也不含第三方统计或跟踪工具。应用使用的唯一第三方服务是第 5 节所述的订阅服务，购买前需要通过家长验证。

3. How Information Is Used · 信息使用方式

The records described in Section 1 are used only inside the app, to show progress and the parent's mood and focus log. We do not sell, share or transfer these records to anyone. Purchase information described in Section 5 is used only to provide and manage your subscription.

第 1 节所述的记录仅在应用内使用，用于显示进度以及家长页的情绪与专注记录。我们不会出售、共享或转让这些记录。第 5 节所述的购买信息仅用于提供和管理您的订阅。

4. Data Storage and Retention · 数据存储与保留

The records described in Section 1 are stored locally on your own device. They are not uploaded to any server or cloud service, and the developer cannot see them. Records stay on the device until you delete the app or clear its data, which permanently removes them.

第 1 节所述的记录只保存在您自己的设备（手机）本地，不会上传到任何服务器或云服务，开发者也无法查看。记录会一直保留在设备上，直到您卸载应用或清除应用数据，届时记录将被永久删除。

5. Purchases and Subscriptions · 购买与订阅

Some features are available through an optional paid subscription. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details; Google handles billing under its own privacy policy.

To confirm whether a subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for the app, your purchase and subscription status (such as product and renewal or expiry dates), and basic technical information needed to process the purchase. It does not receive your name, email address, or the records in Section 1. RevenueCat handles this information under its own privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy).

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Any free trial, price and billing period are shown before you confirm a purchase. You can cancel at any time in Google Play (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions); cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You can restore a purchase on a new device with the "Restore purchases" button.

部分功能通过可选的付费订阅提供。购买由 Google Play 处理。我们不会接收或保存您的银行卡等支付信息，计费由 Google 按其自身隐私政策处理。

为确认订阅是否有效，应用使用订阅管理服务 RevenueCat。RevenueCat 会收到为应用生成的匿名标识符、您的购买与订阅状态（例如商品、续订或到期日期），以及处理购买所需的基本技术信息。它不会收到您的姓名、电子邮件地址，也不会收到第 1 节所述的记录。RevenueCat 按其自身隐私政策处理这些信息（revenuecat.com/privacy）。

订阅会自动续订，直至您取消。免费试用（如有）、价格和计费周期会在您确认购买前显示。您可以随时在 Google Play 中取消（Play 商店 → 个人资料 → 付款和订阅 → 订阅）；取消在当前计费周期结束时生效。更换设备后，可通过"恢复购买"按钮找回订阅。

6. Your Rights · 您的权利

Because the records described in Section 1 stay on your device, you control them: you can delete them at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. Purchase information is not stored on your device; to ask about or request deletion of it, contact us at starparkasd428@gmail.com and we will help you through the subscription service and Google Play. If you have any other question about this policy, contact us at the same address.

由于第 1 节所述的记录保存在您的设备上，您可以自行控制：随时卸载应用或清除应用数据即可删除。购买信息不保存在您的设备上；如需咨询或申请删除，请联系 starparkasd428@gmail.com，我们会协助您通过订阅服务和 Google Play 处理。如对本政策有任何其他疑问，也请联系该邮箱。
